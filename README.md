# RE:WILD

> **Four animals. Four lives. One changing climate.**  
> *Every life ends. Some ends are written by the weather.*

**RE:WILD** is a multi-chapter ecological anthology game developed in **48 hours** by a team of five using **Unity 6**. Players experience a series of short lives as four different animals—a **Frog**, a **Beaver**, a **Bee**, and a **Monkey**—each confronting an existential environmental crisis driven by climate change. Between chapters, a reincarnation light carries your spirit into the next creature's story, concluding each chapter with an environmental reflection on the species' real-world plight.

## Chapters & Gameplay

### Chapter 1: The Frog — *by Zeynep Koz*

Eat flies, plant seeds, and turn barren mud green again. Feed **3 starving companions** and sow **3 seeds** to restore life to the drying pond.

* **WASD**: Move & turn
* **Space**: Jump
* **Shift**: Sprint
* **Left Click**: Catch flies
* **Right Click**: Plant seed
* **E**: Croak / Interact
* **F**: Feed a friend

### Chapter 2: The Beaver — *by Emirhan Kavgacı*

The river is losing water by the second under a relentless drought. Scour the riverbanks, pick up debris, and carry materials to construct a **5×4 block dam (20 pieces total)** before the riverbed runs dry. Human trash only buys temporary time, while natural branches and logs hold back the water far more effectively and restore the stream.

* **WASD**: Move & Strafe
* **Mouse**: 360° Camera & Character Look
* **Shift**: Sprint
* **Space**: Jump
* **Left Click (Hold & Release)**: Pick up, carry, and place repair items into the dam
* **R**: Quick Restart

### Chapter 3: The Bee — *by Özlem Göksün*

Feed the hive before the clock strikes zero, but balance your harvest with care. Strip the flowers bare and the meadow collapses; harvest too conservatively and your colony starves. Pollination is a fragile equilibrium.

* **WASD / Mouse**: Flight navigation
* **Left Click / Space**: Pollinate & collect nectar

### Chapter 4: The Monkey — *by Hasan Parlatır*

You are a Black-headed Squirrel Monkey, and the floodwaters are rising fast behind you. Sprint, vault, and swing through the Amazonian canopy in a high-speed, first-person parkour chase spanning Easy, Medium, and Hard zones. The foliage withers before your eyes—shifting from lush green to dry brittle brown, and finally to a drowned grey-blue.

* **WASD / Mouse**: Move / Look
* **Shift / C**: Sprint / Ground slide
* **Space**: Jump / Wall jump / Branch swing launch
* **W / S (on a branch)**: Swing back and forth
* **A / D (on a branch)**: Slide along the branch
* **E**: Transfer to a nearby branch
* **Left Ctrl**: Drop / Release branch

## Ecological Context

Each chapter in **RE:WILD** reflects a real ecological crisis caused by shifting climate patterns:

* **The Frog (Wetland Loss)**: Amphibians are among the most climate-vulnerable vertebrates on Earth; shrinking ponds and drying mudflats directly disrupt their breeding cycles and local food webs.
* **The Beaver (Drought & Waterway Pollution)**: Prolonged droughts are drying up streams and wetlands worldwide. As natural wood becomes scarce in polluted waterways, beavers are increasingly forced to incorporate human plastic and waste into their dams—materials that fail to retain water the way natural timber does.
* **The Bee (Phenological Mismatch & Scarcity)**: Shifting seasons and extreme weather disrupt the delicate timing between flowering plants and pollinators, threatening both wild ecosystems and global food security.
* **The Monkey (Intensifying Amazonian Floods)**: The Black-headed Squirrel Monkey (*Saimiri vanzolinii*) survives exclusively in a small pocket of flooded *várzea* forest in the Brazilian Amazon. While adapted to seasonal floods, disrupted rainfall cycles and intensifying extremes threaten to submerge their fragile refuge permanently. The IUCN classifies the species as **Endangered**, with population declines projected to exceed 50% over the next three decades.

> *We cannot stop a climate catastrophe with a single leap. Neither can an animal alone.*
